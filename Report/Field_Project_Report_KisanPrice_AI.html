Chapter 1: Introduction

Agriculture represents the foundation of India's rural economy. In Western Maharashtra, districts such as Nashik, Pune, Ahmednagar, and Sangli serve as agricultural powerhouses supplying perishable vegetables, grains, and cash crops to major urban consumption centers across India. Despite modern telecommunications, the wholesale marketing of agricultural commodities remains bound to physical wholesale yards known as Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMC).

At these yards, price discovery takes place through manual open auctions conducted by commission agents (Adatdars). Farmers harvest their crops with virtually no real-time transparency regarding prices in neighboring or terminal mandis. Often, a farmer sells onions in Lasalgaon for ₹2,800 per quintal while the Vashi Navi Mumbai terminal mandi—just 180 km away—trades at ₹3,600 per quintal. Without clear calculations of transit freight deductions, farmers are unable to exploit these market spreads.

KisanPrice AI is engineered as a modern, web-first agricultural intelligence platform. Built with Next.js 16, TypeScript, React 19, and Tailwind CSS, it tracks 104 commodities across 10 agricultural categories, calculates inter-mandi price spreads with transportation cost deductions, generates 7-day AI econometric price forecasts, and automatically detects nearest mandis using GPS coordinates.

Chapter 2: Information Gathering & Field Research

Field research was conducted across 30 field hours during September and October 2026 at two major agricultural trade centers: the Pune Gultekdi APMC Mandi and the Lasalgaon APMC Mandi (Nashik District).

The research methodology combined direct morning auction observations, semi-structured interviews with 15 registered commission agents (Adatdars) and 5 APMC market officials, and a comprehensive survey administered to 50 local farmers.

  • 84% of surveyed farmers reported having no digital tool to verify prevailing prices prior to harvesting.
  • 78% of farmers dispatched produce without knowing the auction price, learning it only after produce was unloaded.
  • 92% of farmers expressed an urgent need for 3- to 7-day price forecasts to decide harvesting schedules.
  • 88% of farmers own an Android smartphone with 4G/5G mobile connectivity.

Chapter 3: Analysis of Existing System

The existing system relies on manual chalkboards, paper tokens, and word-of-mouth quotes. Government portals such as Agmarknet suffer from a 24- to 48-hour delay in publishing verified modal prices, rendering them ineffective for morning trading decisions.

Furthermore, existing tools do not compute transportation costs. A higher price in a distant mandi is meaningless if vehicle hire charges exceed the price differential. KisanPrice AI directly bridges this gap through automated net-in-hand arbitrage calculations.

Chapter 4: Problem Statement

"Small and marginal farmers across Indian agricultural mandis suffer severe economic losses due to localized price blindness, lack of inter-market price comparison, and the absence of freight-adjusted net return calculations. Consequently, farmers frequently fall victim to distress selling at local APMC yards during post-harvest gluts, while consuming terminal markets trade at premiums of 20% to 35%. There is a critical, unmet need for an intelligent, lightweight, and accessible agricultural decision support platform that delivers real-time mandi benchmarking, transparent freight net-gain calculations, and 7-day predictive price trajectory forecasts without relying on heavy external network assets."

Chapter 5: Objectives & Scope

  • Multi-Mandi Surveillance: Real-time benchmarking across 16 major APMC mandis in Maharashtra and key national terminals.
  • 104-Commodity Catalog: 10 agricultural categories styled with 100% self-contained SVG vector graphics for zero network lag.
  • Inter-Mandi Arbitrage & Freight Calculator: Interactive transit slider (default ₹65/quintal/100km) computing net returns.
  • 7-Day AI Price Forecast: Econometric LSTM trajectory with Bullish/Bearish sentiment badges and 4 agricultural market drivers.
  • Geolocation Nearest Mandi Discovery: Haversine proximity calculations identifying closest APMC in kilometers.

Chapter 7: Technical Implementation

The platform was implemented using Next.js 16.4, React 19, TypeScript 5.7, Tailwind CSS v4, Drizzle ORM, and Recharts.

7.1 Core Mathematical Formulations

1. Haversine Distance Formula:

d = 2 * R * asin( sqrt( sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat1) * cos(lat2) * sin²(Δlon/2) ) ) (where R = 6,371 km)

2. Net-In-Hand Arbitrage Formula:

Freight Expense = (Distance in km / 100) * TransportCostParameter Net Return per Quintal = ModalPrice - Freight Expense
